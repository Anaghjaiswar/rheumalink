What we treat

Complex conditions, explained plainly

Joint and autoimmune conditions rarely show up as a single symptom. We look at the whole pattern — labs, imaging, and how you actually move — before we name anything.

Rheumatoid arthritis

Autoimmune joint inflammation, typically symmetric, that can erode cartilage and bone without early treatment.

Lupus (SLE)

A systemic autoimmune disease that can affect skin, joints, kidneys, and blood — we coordinate care across all of it.

Gout

Sudden, severe joint attacks driven by uric acid crystals — we manage flares and the metabolics behind them.

Osteoarthritis

Cartilage wear at load-bearing joints. We combine imaging with movement assessment to slow its progress.

Ankylosing spondylitis

Inflammatory stiffening of the spine and pelvis. Early diagnosis keeps mobility on the table longer.

Psoriatic arthritis

Joint and skin disease that travel together — treatment plans address both at once, not separately.

How a first visit unfolds

Four visits, one continuous read on your joints

Autoimmune disease shifts over time, so the process doesn't end at diagnosis — it repeats, deliberately, for as long as you need us.

01

Consult

We map your symptom history, family history, and what a bad day actually feels like — in your words first.

02

Assess

Bloodwork, imaging, and a hands-on joint exam, including goniometry to measure exact range of motion.

03

Diagnose

We walk through the differential with you, plainly, before we commit to a label or a treatment path.

04

Treat & monitor

A plan built around your flares, with scheduled check-ins to adjust it as your disease — or your life — changes.
